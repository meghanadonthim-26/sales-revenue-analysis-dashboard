# 📊 Sales & Revenue Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was developed as part of my **Data Analytics Internship at Thiranex**.

The **Sales & Revenue Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Excel-based dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, revenue trends, product performance, and regional performance.

The dashboard uses sales data to generate meaningful KPIs, visualizations, filters, and business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
- Identify monthly revenue trends.
- Find the top-performing products.
- Compare revenue across different categories.
- Analyze revenue performance across regions.
- Provide interactive filtering using slicers.
- Generate useful business insights from the sales data.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

- **Microsoft Excel**
- **PivotTables**
- **PivotCharts**
- **Slicers**
- **Excel Tables**
- **Data Analysis & Visualization**

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset contains **250 sales records** with information such as:

- Order ID
- Order Date
- Region
- Category
- Product
- Customer
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Discount
- Sales
- Revenue

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes the following KPIs:

- **Total Sales**
- **Total Revenue**
- **Total Orders**
- **Total Quantity Sold**

---

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard contains:

### 1. Monthly Revenue Trend
A line chart showing how revenue changes throughout the year.

### 2. Revenue by Category
A column chart comparing revenue generated by different product categories.

### 3. Top 5 Products by Revenue
A chart highlighting the five products generating the highest revenue.

### 4. Revenue by Region
A comparison of revenue generated across different geographical regions.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive slicers for:

- **Region**
- **Category**
- **Product**

These filters allow users to interact with the dashboard and analyze specific segments of the sales data.

---

## 💡 Business Insights

The dashboard helps identify:

- Monthly variations in revenue and sales performance.
- High-performing products that contribute significantly to revenue.
- Strong and weak product categories.
- High-performing and underperforming regions.
- Areas where marketing and inventory strategies can be improved.

### Business Recommendations

- Prioritize high-performing products for inventory availability.
- Focus marketing campaigns on strong-performing regions.
- Analyze weaker categories to understand their performance.
- Monitor monthly revenue trends to identify opportunities and slow periods.
- Use data-driven insights to improve sales and revenue strategies.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download the Excel file from this repository.
2. Open it using Microsoft Excel.
3. Go to the **Dashboard** sheet.
4. Use the Region, Category, and Product slicers to filter the data.
5. Analyze the KPIs, charts, and business insights.
